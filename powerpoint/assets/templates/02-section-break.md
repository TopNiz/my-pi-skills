# Slide 2: Section Break

## Description
A dark section divider centers a globe above three ESG cubes. “Section Break” is placed prominently, with a subtitle below. A broad dark band anchors the lower part of the slide.

## Geometric language
The visual relies on a centered vertical stack: globe, block-like ESG cubes, title, and subtitle. The lower rectangular band separates the divider from the next section and gives the slide a strong baseline.

## Useful when
Use it to introduce a new chapter, such as Environmental, Social, Governance, methodology, results, or recommendations.
