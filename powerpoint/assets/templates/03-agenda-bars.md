# Slide 3: Agenda Bars

## Description
A photo of the glowing bulb occupies the left side. On the right, four numbered agenda items are arranged vertically, each with a long colored horizontal bar and a short text block.

## Geometric language
The main shapes are four stacked rectangles with slightly contrasting accent colors, small numbered text boxes, and grouped content boxes aligned to the right. Equal spacing creates rhythm; the varying colors distinguish sections without changing the overall structure.

## Useful when
Use it for an agenda, workplan, presentation roadmap, or list of four strategic priorities.
