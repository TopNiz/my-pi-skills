# Slide 4: Roadmap Timeline

## Description
A six-year roadmap runs down the left and center of the slide, from 2022 through 2027. Rounded cards alternate around a vertical line, with colored circular nodes marking each year. A large title and explanatory text sit on the right.

## Geometric language
The diagram combines a vertical connector line, small circular milestones, alternating rounded rectangles, and short horizontal connectors. The alternating placement reduces overlap and makes the chronology easy to scan.

## Useful when
Use it for implementation phases, annual targets, policy milestones, product releases, or a multi-year ESG transition plan.
