# Slide 5: Team Structure

## Description
A central rounded card sits above three lower cards for roles such as Director, Marketing, and Design. Connector lines form a simple organization chart. Photo placeholders and a larger top placeholder can personalize the team.

## Geometric language
The structure uses a rounded rectangle and pentagon-like header for the central role, three repeated rounded cards below, straight connectors, and small rectangular information bands. Repetition communicates equal status among the three branches.

## Useful when
Use it for an organization chart, project team, governance model, responsibility split, or ESG steering committee.
