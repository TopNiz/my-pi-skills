# Slide 6: Gear Pillars

## Description
The left side contains three overlapping cog or gear illustrations in green, blue, and yellow. The right side contains three aligned text columns, while three colored content cards run along the bottom.

## Geometric language
The infographic combines circular gear silhouettes, arcs, small freeform connector elements, and repeated rectangular cards. Overlap suggests interdependence, while the three colors create a three-part system.

## Useful when
Use it to explain three mutually reinforcing capabilities, ESG pillars, operating principles, or linked workstreams.
