# Slide 7: ESG Framework

## Description
A large illustrated globe, clouds, people, trees, and a small industrial scene occupy the left half. On the right, three horizontal ESG rows are labeled Environment, Social, and Governance, each followed by explanatory text.

## Geometric language
The slide pairs a complex custom illustration with three repeated horizontal label bars and white text panels. The repeated bars create a clean reading order beside the more organic visual.

## Useful when
Use it as a general ESG overview, stakeholder briefing, or opening explanation of the three dimensions.
