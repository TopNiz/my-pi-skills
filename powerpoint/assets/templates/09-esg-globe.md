# Slide 9: ESG Globe Overview

## Description
Hands support a large globe labeled ESG. Stylized plants and landscape forms create a green foreground. The right side contains three stacked colored labels for Environmental, Social, and Governance with supporting text.

## Geometric language
The bottom is built from irregular freeform landscape shapes, while the right uses repeated rectangular text blocks. The centered globe and symmetrical hands provide a strong emblematic focal point.

## Useful when
Use it for an ESG vision slide, sustainability promise, impact overview, or a transition from a title into the three pillars.
