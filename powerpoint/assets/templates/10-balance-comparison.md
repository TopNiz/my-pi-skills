# Slide 10: Balance Comparison

## Description
A central balance scale contrasts an industrial scene on the left with people, trees, and a greener setting on the right. Green and orange ribbon labels flank the illustration in two columns.

## Geometric language
The slide combines long ribbon or chevron-ended rectangles, a custom balance-scale illustration, circular or organic landscape forms, and repeated text blocks. The scale provides an explicit visual metaphor for trade-offs and balance.

## Useful when
Use it to compare economic and environmental outcomes, competing options, risks and benefits, or the balance between industrial activity and social value.
