# Slide 12: Impact Metrics

## Description
A globe with ESG lettering is centered above a stylized city and landscape skyline. Three colored KPI blocks display example values such as $130, $200, and $80, each paired with a label and explanatory copy.

## Geometric language
The composition uses a large circular globe, layered organic landscape silhouettes, a linear city skyline, and three repeated rectangular metric cards. The cards create a simple comparison row beneath the main symbol.

## Useful when
Use it for impact metrics, investment cases, financial benefits, sustainability outcomes, or a three-number executive summary.
