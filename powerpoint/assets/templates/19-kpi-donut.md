# Slide 19: KPI Donut

## Description
A central circular percentage graphic displays 48.6%, with colored ring segments and a small center label. Three surrounding KPI callouts repeat the percentage and add text descriptions.

## Geometric language
The visual uses concentric circles, separated donut segments, radial markers, and small connector or label elements. Repeated percentage callouts let one central KPI be interpreted through several perspectives.

## Useful when
Use it for performance rates, progress toward a target, survey results, completion percentages, or three related KPI narratives.
