# Slide 20: Mobile Portfolio

## Description
Two overlapping smartphone illustrations dominate the left side, each showing a large question mark. The right side contains a portfolio title and description. Three icons at the bottom represent environmental, social, and governance content categories.

## Geometric language
The composition uses two tilted rounded phone rectangles, oversized question-mark text, a right-hand text column, and three icon-and-label units separated by vertical rules. Overlap adds depth and indicates a mobile experience.

## Useful when
Use it for a digital product portfolio, mobile app concept, service catalogue, or a slide introducing three content modules.
