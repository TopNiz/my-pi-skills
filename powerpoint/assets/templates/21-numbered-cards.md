# Slide 21: Numbered Cards

## Description
Six large numbered cards are arranged in two columns and three rows. Each card has a colored background, a large numeral at the left edge, a title, and a short paragraph.

## Geometric language
The cards are rounded parallelogram-like rectangles with small angled ends and oversized numerals layered into the left side. The six-unit grid supports a clear scan from 1 to 6.

## Useful when
Use it for six recommendations, a project checklist, a six-stage method, or a set of ESG actions.
