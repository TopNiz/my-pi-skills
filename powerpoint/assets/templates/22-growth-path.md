# Slide 22: Growth Path

## Description
Three horizontal colored bands carry stages 01, 02, and 03. A tall green upward arrow or house-like pillar runs through the center and contains three circular icons, suggesting growth and progression.

## Geometric language
The design combines full-width horizontal rectangles, a central upward arrow, three circular icon containers, and small vertical separators. The central arrow turns parallel stages into one shared trajectory.

## Useful when
Use it for maturity levels, a three-stage roadmap, capability development, or the progression from portfolio to presentation to modern delivery.
