# Slide 24: Step Arrows

## Description
Three ascending arrow forms are labeled 01, 02, and 03. Each arrow carries a colored horizontal chevron, while side text boxes explain the step. Small dotted connectors show the reading direction.

## Geometric language
The diagram uses large upward arrows, overlaid right-pointing chevrons, bordered rectangular callouts, and dotted horizontal lines. The rising positions communicate increasing maturity or progress.

## Useful when
Use it for a three-step action plan, maturity model, staged implementation, or a sequence that must show both order and advancement.
