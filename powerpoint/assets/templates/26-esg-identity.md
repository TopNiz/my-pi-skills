# Slide 26: ESG Identity

## Description
A large abstract ESG or question-mark form sits inside a row of pale segmented vertical panels. The lower area contains a yellow text block on the left, a large orange statement on the right, and three colored tags: PowerPoint, Presentation, and Infographic.

The upper graphic uses repeated vertical panels with slight changes in angle or width, creating a folded or panoramic effect. The bottom uses rectangular text blocks and three small label rectangles as category markers.

## Useful when
Use it for a conceptual title slide, brand or identity statement, or an overview that classifies content into three formats.
