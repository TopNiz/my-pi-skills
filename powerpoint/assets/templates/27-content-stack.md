# Slide 27: Content Stack

## Description
Four colored content blocks labeled A through D are stacked on a tall gray upward arrow. Dotted horizontal connectors point left to four matching explanatory text rows.

## Geometric language
The slide uses a vertical arrow or pillar, four repeated ribbon-like rectangles, and dotted bidirectional mapping lines. The stack communicates accumulation or hierarchy, while the lines connect each layer to its explanation.

## Useful when
Use it for a four-layer model, portfolio architecture, workstream stack, or mapping content categories to their descriptions.
