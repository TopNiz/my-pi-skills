# Slide 30: SWOT Wheel

## Description
Four large rounded quadrants surround a central circular globe graphic labeled SWOT and ESG. The quadrants are color-coded blue, yellow, green, and orange, with one text area for each SWOT category.

## Geometric language
The structure combines four rounded rectangular panels, four quarter-ring segments, a central circle, and a faint globe illustration. The center acts as a common reference point while the four quadrants separate the analysis.

## Useful when
Use it for a SWOT analysis, ESG risk scan, strategic diagnosis, or a four-part assessment around one central theme.
