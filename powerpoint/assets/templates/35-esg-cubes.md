# Slide 35: ESG Cubes

## Description
A hand places an ESG cube onto a row of other cubes in a dark photographic scene. Three small cubes or icons represent Environmental, Social, and Governance, while “Infographic Style” appears near the bottom.

## Geometric language
The cube blocks provide a tangible geometric metaphor for building or assembling a system. The repeated square faces and horizontal row communicate modularity; the hand adds an action cue.

## Useful when
Use it to show assembling an ESG strategy, adding a new capability, governance ownership, or building a portfolio step by step.
