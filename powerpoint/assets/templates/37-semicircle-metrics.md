# Slide 37: Semicircle Metrics

## Description
Three separate semicircular gauges display percentage splits of 30/70, 70/30, and 16/84. Each gauge has a colored percentage label and a short metric or monetary amount beneath it.

## Geometric language
The visual uses three half-donut or semicircle charts, contrasting segment colors, center labels, and repeated captions. The separate gauges support side-by-side comparison without requiring a shared axis.

## Useful when
Use it for three ratio KPIs, demographic or stakeholder splits, portfolio allocation, or comparing proportions across scenarios.
