# Slide 43: Thank You

## Description
The closing slide returns to the dark photograph of a hand holding a glowing lightbulb. “THANK YOU” is centered to the right of the image, with a subtitle below.

## Geometric language
The full-bleed rectangular photo is paired with a simple centered text block and generous negative space. The restrained geometry creates a clean ending rather than another information diagram.

## Useful when
Use it as the final slide, especially for presentations about innovation, transformation, sustainability, or future opportunities.
