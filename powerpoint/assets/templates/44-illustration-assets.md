# Slide 44: Illustration Assets

## Description
An asset board displays a broad collection of editable ESG illustrations: globe and weather scenes, gears, people, an industrial facility, a city, a tree, hands, and ESG blocks. The slide is titled “Fully Editable Shapes”.

## Geometric language
The board is a collage of grouped vector illustrations and freeform organic shapes, shown against a checkerboard-style transparency background. The collection mixes circles, blocks, gear teeth, skyline rectangles, leaves, and irregular silhouettes.

## Useful when
Use it as a source slide when building custom ESG diagrams. Copy individual grouped illustrations into other slides and recolor or resize them as needed.
