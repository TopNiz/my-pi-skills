# Slide 45: Editable Graphics

## Description
A second asset board presents large reusable graphics: a globe with clouds and ESG blocks, a smartphone, a laptop, a gear-and-people scene, a tree, hands, a city, and a globe icon.

## Geometric language
The slide combines grouped illustrations with circular globes, rounded device rectangles, cloud shapes, organic leaves, building blocks, and small icon clusters. The transparent background emphasizes that these are components rather than a finished layout.

## Useful when
Use it as a visual library for assembling a custom cover, digital transformation slide, ESG framework, or portfolio page.
